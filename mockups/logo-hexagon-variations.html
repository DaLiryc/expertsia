ExpertsIA - Original Wordmark + Hexagon Variations

My Take

Here's what I think from a design and branding perspective:

  • The hexagon is a winner - It represents stability, precision, and tech-forward thinking without being generic like circles or squares. It's uncommon enough to stand out while still feeling professional.
  • Color strategy works - Your terracotta (#e07b39) and forest green (#2d5a3d) combo is distinctive. Most AI sites use blue/purple, so this feels fresh and grounded.
  • Balance needed - The "E" monogram inside a hexagon is good, but might feel too small as a standalone favicon. Consider either: keeping the "E" but making it bolder, OR using the hexagon shape alone as a symbol.
  • Wordmark vs Icon - For a consulting agency, the wordmark is primary. The hexagon/icon should complement it, not compete. The best logos work at both full size and tiny (favicon).

My recommendation: Go with a clean hexagon symbol (no letters inside) paired with the minimalist wordmark. The symbol can be used alone for favicons, app icons, and social avatars. The colored "IA" in the wordmark reinforces the AI aspect. This gives you maximum flexibility.
